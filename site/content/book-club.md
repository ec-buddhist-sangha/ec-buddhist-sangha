  ### What is the Majjhima Nikāya?

  *Majjhima Nikāya* means “Collection of Middle Length Discourses.” Its 152 suttas record teachings and conversations of the Buddha and his disciples, covering meditation, ethical conduct, and understanding suffering.

  It belongs to the **Pali Canon**, the collection of scriptures preserved in the Theravāda tradition. Also called the *Tipiṭaka*, or “three baskets,” the canon has three main divisions:

  - **Vinaya Piṭaka:** monastic rules and the stories behind them.
  - **Sutta Piṭaka:** discourses of the Buddha and his disciples. This is where our book belongs.
  - **Abhidhamma Piṭaka:** systematic analyses of mind, experience, and the teachings.

  **Pali Canon → Sutta Piṭaka → Majjhima Nikāya**

  Within the Sutta Piṭaka, there are five collections, called *nikāyas*:

  - **Dīgha Nikāya (DN):** the long discourses.
  - **Majjhima Nikāya (MN):** the middle length discourses we are reading.
  - **Saṃyutta Nikāya (SN):** discourses grouped by theme.
  - **Aṅguttara Nikāya (AN):** discourses arranged by numbered groups of teachings, such as the five faculties or seven factors of awakening.
  - **Khuddaka Nikāya (KN):** a varied collection of texts, including the *Dhammapada*, *Sutta Nipāta*, and verses of the elder monks and nuns.

  These collections often return to the same teachings in different settings. You don't need to read them in order. A reference such as **MN 10** simply means the tenth discourse in the Majjhima Nikāya.

  For more background, see Access to Insight's guides to the [Pali Canon](https://accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/index.html) and the [five collections of discourses](https://accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/sutta.html).

  ### Why the Majjhima Nikāya?

  For our book club, these discourses offer a useful balance of depth and manageable weekly readings. Their conversations and stories cover a wide range of teachings, giving us plenty to discuss. Clear Mountain's matching videos provide guidance along the way. Some suttas are challenging, and that's okay: we can explore one question or connection to practice without needing to understand everything at once.

Inspired by Clear Mountain Monastery's ongoing [Sunday Sutta Study](https://www.clearmountain.org/events/sunday-sutta-study/), we'll read the **152 Middle Length Discourses of the Buddha (Majjhima Nikāya)** alongside their **10-Minute Majjhima** videos, one sutta each week over about three years. We'll talk about how the teachings relate to our lives and meditation practice.

### How we meet

**7–7:30 p.m.: Book club.** Read the week's sutta beforehand on SuttaCentral. On Zoom, we'll watch Clear Mountain Monastery's **10-Minute Majjhima** video for that sutta, then discuss it.

**7:30–8 p.m.: Sangha meet-and-greet.** Stay for a casual chat, or join us at 7:30 just to say hello and get to know others in the sangha. Everyone is welcome, whether or not you're interested in the book club. No reading or preparation is needed for this part.

We meet on Thursdays, beginning November 5, 2026. All times are Central. Register once using the link above and use the same Zoom link for either part of the evening.

### Joining in

**You can join at any point.** Each week focuses on one sutta, so you don't need to catch up on earlier meetings. No prior experience is needed, and questions are welcome.

### Reading and watching

All readings are [free on SuttaCentral](https://suttacentral.net/pitaka/sutta/middle/mn). You don't need to buy a book. If you prefer a printed copy, the edition pictured is *The Middle Length Discourses of the Buddha*, translated by Bhikkhu Ñāṇamoli and Bhikkhu Bodhi, published by [Wisdom Publications](https://wisdomexperience.org/product/middle-length-discourses-buddha/).

The [10-Minute Majjhima videos](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLomY-Sp4p5YsIVG0DmvJhUcR6YaXR_3wf) are free on YouTube. We'll watch together during the meeting, so there's no need to watch ahead.

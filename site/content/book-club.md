  ### About the Majjhima Nikāya

  The *Majjhima Nikāya* (Middle Length Discourses) contains 152 teachings and conversations from the Pali Canon. For more background, see [SuttaCentral's introduction to the collection](https://suttacentral.net/pitaka/sutta/middle/mn) and Access to Insight's [guide to the Pali Canon](https://accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/index.html).

Inspired by Clear Mountain Monastery's [Sunday Sutta Study](https://www.clearmountain.org/events/sunday-sutta-study/), we're reading the **Middle Length Discourses of the Buddha** with their **10-Minute Majjhima** videos. One sutta a week, a short teaching, and a conversation about what it means in our own lives.

### Thursday evenings

**7–7:30 p.m. · Sutta study / book club**

Read the week's sutta if you can. We'll watch the video together, then talk about what stood out, what puzzled us, and what we might put into practice.

**7:30–8 p.m. · Sangha social**

Join us for a chat, with or without the book club.

*If no one else shows up, enjoy a little quiet time with your one-person sangha.*

All times are Central, starting **November 5, 2026**. Register once above; the same Zoom link works for both.

### Join any week

We're reading all 152 suttas over about three years. Come when you can: each week has its own reading, with no catching up required. No prior experience needed.

### Read for free, or bring a book

Every reading is [free on SuttaCentral](https://suttacentral.net/pitaka/sutta/middle/mn). For a paper copy, we use *The Middle Length Discourses of the Buddha*, translated by Bhikkhu Ñāṇamoli and Bhikkhu Bodhi, from [Wisdom Publications](https://wisdomexperience.org/product/middle-length-discourses-buddha/).

The [10-Minute Majjhima videos](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLomY-Sp4p5YsIVG0DmvJhUcR6YaXR_3wf) are free too. We'll watch together, so there's no need to watch ahead.

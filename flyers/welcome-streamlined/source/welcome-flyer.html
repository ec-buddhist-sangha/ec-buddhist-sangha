Arriving & Getting Settled

If you arrive after 7:00, we will probably already be meditating, but you are still warmly encouraged to join us. Please enter quietly and take any open seat. Chairs are available. Large cushions are under the table inside the hall; small cushions and blankets are in the closet.

  1. 6:45 PMOptional orientation to sitting practice
  2. 7:00 PMSilent sitting meditation
  3. 7:30 PMDharma talk, Buddhist teaching, or discussion
  4. 8:00 PMQuestions, reflections, and community conversation
